# Sandport artwork

The harbor atlas incorporates color-graded photographs from Poly Haven under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). The materials are [Worn Plaster Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/worn_plaster_wall), [Seaworn Sandstone Brick](https://polyhaven.com/a/seaworn_sandstone_brick), [Cobblestone Floor 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_05), [Red Sandstone Pavement](https://polyhaven.com/a/red_sandstone_pavement), and [Weathered Brown Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/weathered_brown_planks).

The two transparent training sprites were generated for this game. The articulated opponent uses a clothing and equipment atlas derived from the original costume illustration, with painted seams, webbing and surface wear.
